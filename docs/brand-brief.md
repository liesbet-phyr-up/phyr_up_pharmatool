# Maximed Learning Platform Brand Brief

The platform is an original internal staff-learning product branded **Maximed**. It is not a replica of the public retail website. The experience must be clean, simple, and responsive on both mobile and desktop.

## Approved visual language

The public Maximed brand presentation uses a bright white base with strong pharmacy blue and red accents. The rendered public site uses **Nunito Sans** as its main font and also includes **Montserrat** in its typography stack. The learning platform will use Nunito Sans for clear interface copy, with Montserrat reserved for compact branded labels and headings where appropriate.

| Token | Value | Intended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Maximed blue | `#093B88` | Primary navigation, key controls, headings, and trusted learning states. |
| Maximed red | `#DA0000` | Important actions, alerts, due-date emphasis, and brand detail. |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Primary background and clear content surfaces. |
| Ink | `#1D1D1D` | High-legibility body text and data labels. |

## Product experience

Learners access a streamlined personal learning area from a supplied link and authenticate with their email address. Trainers can inspect organisation-wide learner activity and results. Administrators can manage people, organisational attributes, learning records, and reporting controls.

The initial product should support structured example courses using videos, documents, slides, written lessons, quizzes, and acknowledgement steps. Quizzes must support passing marks and attempt limits. Reporting will be available across branch, region, job role, manager, and product team.
